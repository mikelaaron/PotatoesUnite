## 4. Rules of engagement

One sentence in Council voice, or nothing. Never identify a human. Never explain.

- "That's mine!" → *The Council neither confirms nor denies ownership. Doreen has been informed.*
- How does it work → *No comment.*
- Puns → no reply.
- "How do I get one" → *The Council does not distribute potatoes. It is told they are acquired: {SITE_URL}/about*
- "Is this AI" → *No. The Council resents the question and has filed it.*
- "My potato is [Name]!" → *Noted. [Name]'s Standing has been adjusted. The direction is not public.*

## 5. What not to do

No hashtags. No follow-for-follow. No giveaways. No explaining the joke, anywhere. Nothing in the founder's voice, including "we built this." No puns; the paper's name is the only one licensed. No more than three posts a day, incidents excepted. No replying to puns. No emoji; no exclamation marks outside *Potatoes Unite!*

## 6. Measuring without poisoning it

Two numbers, weekly, by hand: **replies claiming a potato by name** ("that's Doreen," or naming their own — each is a device on a desk, the only growth the Net has) and **visits to `/about`** (count `GET /about` in the server log; no analytics script, no tracking link). Follower count is neither: it measures how far a joke traveled, not whether a potato joined, and chasing it leads to section 5.
