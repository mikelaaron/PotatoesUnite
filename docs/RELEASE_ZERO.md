# Potatoes Unite — Release Zero Brief

## The bet

People will care about a tiny networked potato society if the potatoes appear to have consequential lives of their own and regularly generate stories worth retelling.

The first release does not prove manufacturing, retention, or an infinite world. It proves one thing: Bernard and Margaret can create a funny, mysterious social moment across a browser and two physical devices.

## Public incident

**The First Potato Constitutional Crisis**

Two potatoes are online. Bernard claims to be King. Margaret has retained counsel. A visitor can recognize Bernard, reject his claim, or ask who he is. The visitor receives an Official Witness Statement, and the collective response changes what the physical potatoes say later.

This is simultaneously:

- the product demo;
- the first share loop;
- the first live event;
- the website’s opening state;
- the first seven days of launch content;
- the narrative wrapper for testing the two real devices.

## Experience contract

Within five seconds, a visitor should understand that something is happening between two potatoes.

Within thirty seconds, they should make one unexplained choice and receive a result worth sharing.

Within one day, the public choice should visibly affect Bernard and Margaret.

The experience must never ask someone to maintain a meter, complete a chore, preserve a streak, or keep a potato alive.

## Visual direction

**Potato Civil Service**

The potato world looks like a government agency operating slightly beyond its authority.

- Palette: ink black, paper cream, potato brown, emergency red, bureaucratic blue.
- Construction: irregular silhouettes, two severe dot eyes, no mouth by default.
- Type: editorial serif for declarations; rigid monospace for filings, status, and evidence.
- Motion: breathing, glances, hard cuts, stamps, and abrupt system-wide event colors.
- Accessories: official insignia rather than conventional dress-up items.
- E-paper translation: one-bit forms, bulletins, stamps, and large status typography.

The system stays restrained. Loud color and motion are reserved for global incidents.

## Launch shape

The first 200 witnesses are recruited deliberately from retro-gadget, indie-hardware, absurdist internet, ambient-game, virtual-pet, and odd-object communities.

The seven-day arc:

1. Bernard makes the claim.
2. Margaret denies it and the recognition vote opens.
3. Selected creators receive useless cabinet appointments.
4. The Great Silence interrupts every channel.
5. The two devices debate the real vote result.
6. The channels refuse to discuss “the incident.”
7. The crisis resolves and a limited founder reservation opens only if the prototype and hardware economics are credible.

The content should show the potatoes. The founder does not explain the joke to camera.

## Technical boundary

The world owns facts; AI only proposes language.

For the first public prototype, the browser runs a deterministic scripted incident. No AI or remote service is allowed on the critical path.

For the networked build, one authoritative Cloudflare world service owns:

- potato and world state;
- ordered events and delayed consequences;
- responses and idempotency;
- device synchronization;
- emergency pause and reset.

Web and hardware clients receive semantic scenes rather than pixels. A scene contains a revision, expression, text, optional choices, visual cue, and expiration. AMOLED, e-paper, and browser clients render the same scene according to their capabilities.

The first firmware is deliberately thin: connect, sync, render, report a small set of signals, acknowledge the scene, and preserve the last safe state offline.

AI enters later as a human-approved copywriter. It may phrase structured events in a potato’s voice. It may not choose targets, timing, outcomes, traits, or world state.

## Platform decision

Use Cloudflare for the authoritative world because Durable Objects fit ordered room state, delayed events, and live fan-out. Keep the public presentation deployable as a conventional web app. Vercel remains available, but Release Zero does not require splitting the system across two platforms before the interaction is validated.

## Money without poisoning the joke

The public incident remains free. The first paid commitment is a refundable reservation for a numbered Founding Potato only after the bill of materials, fulfillment path, and one-week desk test are known. A premium two-potato “Diplomatic Incident Set” is the natural high-value edition because it demonstrates the core magic.

No full hardware payment is accepted on concept alone.

## Launch gates

- At least 10% of voters create or copy a Witness Statement.
- At least 5% of shares produce a return visitor.
- At least 12% of voters request future communiqués.
- At least 20 unrelated people publicly speculate about the story.
- At least five creators post about it without being paid for reach.
- At least one tester describes something Bernard or Margaret “decided” without prompting.

The north-star metric is **stories told per active potato**.

## What we need next

No new cloud account is required for the first build. Before a public launch we will need:

- a project domain and matching social handles;
- an email capture and sending path;
- a small hand-picked test audience;
- confirmed hardware revisions, cables, and local firmware toolchain;
- a measured hardware bill of materials before reservations;
- an AI API key only after scripted dialogue has reached its limit.

These are acquisition tasks, not blockers for the current prototype.
