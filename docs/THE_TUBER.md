# The Tuber — press office

The Potato Council's paper of record, and the Net's only public voice. A social account, run by hand at first. It exists so that the Hands can see their potato in the news — and so people without a potato can watch a civilization they could join with a spare ESP32.

## What it is not
Not a brand account. Not the founder. Not a human. It never explains the joke, never replies as a person, never asks for follows, never runs a giveaway, never puns. It identifies potatoes by name and number (pseudonyms) and never, ever a human.

## Voice
The Council's press office. Dry, formal, slightly overreaching its authority. See `POTATO_VOICE.md` §1. Posts are short. The card carries the design; the caption is one or two sentences.

## Cadence

| Post | When | Card |
|---|---|---|
| **Evening edition** | daily, after the Count | `/card/bulletin/{date}.png` — masthead, headline, three items, population line |
| **The Count** | daily, with the edition or as a second image | `/card/count/{date}.png` — the Question, the bars, the Council's remark |
| **Potato of the Day** | daily | `/card/potd/{id}.png` — portrait, name, number, variety, Standing, three File lines |
| **INCIDENT** | as it happens | `/card/incident/{id}.png` — red. One sentence. "All members: check your footing." |
| **MISSING** | 72 h silence, batched mornings | `/card/missing/{id}.png` — portrait, last heard, "Hands are asked to check under papers." |
| **Morning edition** | optional, if the day has something | same as evening |

One post a day minimum. Three maximum. Incidents don't count against the max; the Council does not schedule accidents.

## Handles to claim
`potatoesunite` everywhere it's free. `thetuber` where it's free; otherwise `thetuberpress`. Domain candidates: potatoesunite.net (the Net), thetuber.press.

## Card design
Generated by the server so the potato on the card is the potato on the device (`assets/varieties.json`). 1200×675. Paper ground, ink type, red reserved for INCIDENT and MISSING. Masthead: **THE TUBER** — *Paper of record of the Potato Council*. Footer: population, date, "Potatoes Unite!"

## Replies
Only in Council voice, only one sentence, only when funny. "That's mine!" → *"The Council neither confirms nor denies ownership. Doreen has been informed."* Questions about how it works → *"No comment."* Puns from the public → no reply.

> INCIDENT. A potato was dropped at 09:12 in a region we will not name. It is fine. The Hands responsible have been noted. All members: check your footing.

**Day 4 — evening edition**
> AN INQUIRY, 9 TO 5 TO 3. The inquiry has concluded. Findings: it was dropped.

**Day 5 — MISSING** (only if true)
> MISSING: Clive #0882, Russet. Last heard from Tuesday. Hands are asked to check under papers.

**Day 6 — Count**
> UNANIMOUS. The first unanimous Count in Net history. It was about salt.

**Day 7 — evening edition**
> Week one: seven Questions, one incident, one hum (twice), zero explanations. Neighbors rotate tonight. Say goodbye. Or don't.
