# Potatoes Unite — Launch Plan

## North star

Ship the smallest public experience that makes someone say, “Mine did something weird. Did yours?” and gives them an immediate way to join the next experiment.

## Go/no-go gates

- **Funny:** five cold viewers understand the premise and at least three laugh or retell the moment.
- **Shareable:** at least 20% of testers generate or copy a potato card without prompting.
- **Wanted:** at least 10% of qualified page visitors join the hardware waitlist.
- **Physical magic:** after living with Bernard and Margaret for one week, we naturally describe at least one event as something the potatoes “decided.”

## Deliberately out of scope for release zero

- Open-ended chat, user-generated global events, native accounts, maps, a full social graph, app stores, manufacturing, payments, and autonomous always-on AI.
